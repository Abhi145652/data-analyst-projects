# Superstore Sales & Profit Dashboard — Tableau

## Project Overview
Interactive Tableau dashboard developed to analyze sales and profit performance across regions, categories, and time periods.

## Key Analysis
- Total Sales & Total Profit
- Average Sales & Average Profit
- Region-wise Sales & Profit
- Year & Month-wise Sales & Profit
- Category-wise performance

## Tools & Techniques
- Tableau
- Calculated Fields
- Filters & Parameters
- Reference Lines
- KPI Cards & Interactive Dashboard
